# 🎧 Audiobook Episode 02: Present Perfect vs Continuous: Jangan Terjebak Rumus Mati

> **Series**: Next Level Stories (Everyday Professional English)  
> **Format**: Tri-Voice Duet Audio Storytelling (Bima & Sarah)  
> **Audio File**: [02-differences-between-perfect-and-perfect-continuous-audiobook.mp3](./02-differences-between-perfect-and-perfect-continuous-audiobook.mp3)  
> **Summary**: Membedakan 'I have done' (hasil tuntas) dan 'I have been doing' (proses berjalan) lewat obrolan sehari-hari.

---

## 🎙️ Podcast Script (Full Dialogue)

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Halo lagi teman-teman! Masih bersama Bima dan Sarah di Next Level Stories.

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Hari ini kita mau membedah salah satu kebingungan terbesar: bedanya Present Perfect dengan Present Perfect Continuous.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... Why do so many learners get confused between 'I have worked' and 'I have been working'?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Because textbooks often teach them as dry mathematical formulas! But in real life, think of it this way: Present Perfect focuses on the finished trophy, while Present Perfect Continuous focuses on the sweat and marathon! Listen to this workplace scene between Leo and Emma."*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Emma sighs: 'Phew! I have been debugging this payment gateway all morning!' Leo asks: 'Did you solve it?' Emma smiles: 'Yes! I have fixed three critical security vulnerabilities!'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Perhatikan perbedaannya, teman-teman. Waktu Emma bilang 'I have been debugging all morning', dia lagi curhat tentang perjuangan dan energinya sepanjang pagi. Tapi waktu dia bilang 'I have fixed three issues', dia bangga menunjukkan hasil pialanya yang sudah beres.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"So... Present Perfect is about the result, and Continuous is about the process?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Exactly, Bima! Present Perfect asks: 'How many things are finished?' while Continuous asks: 'How long have you been spending your energy?' Repeat this line after me: 'I have been learning English for six months, and I have made great progress!'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Tirukan dengan mantap: 'I have been learning English for six months, and I have made great progress!' Keren banget! Kita lanjut ke modul dua.
