## 🎙️ Podcast Script (Full Dialogue)

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Di sesi tata bahasa Modul 4 ini, kita bedah rahasia bahasa jurnalistik: Passive Voice dan Reported Speech.

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Kenapa sih berita di media jarang menyebut 'Saya dengar', tapi selalu 'It was announced that' atau 'The initiative was launched'?

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... Why does news journalism love the passive voice?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Because in news, the event itself is often much more important than who held the microphone! Passive voice creates objective distance. For example: 'Over ten thousand trees were planted across the capital last weekend.' Notice how inspiring the action sounds on its own!"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Tepat sekali. Pola passive voice: to be ditambah Verb 3: 'The project was launched', 'The policy has been updated'.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Let's give our listeners a professional reporting phrase."*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Repeat after me: 'According to recent reports, substantial investments have been allocated to public transit.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Tirukan: 'According to recent reports, substantial investments have been allocated to public transit.' Mantap sekali!
