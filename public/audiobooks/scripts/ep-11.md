## 🎙️ Podcast Script (Full Dialogue)

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Halo semuanya! Di Modul 6 ini, kita belajar seni bercerita paling seru: An Unforgettable Story in Your Life.

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Pengalaman tersesat di stasiun kereta luar negeri, ban bocor tengah malam, atau momen kejutan yang tak terlupakan.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... What makes a personal story truly unforgettable to the audience?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Sensory details and emotional pacing, Bima! Don't just say 'It was cold.' Say: 'The freezing wind was biting through my jacket.' Listen to Kevin recounting his hiking adventure: 'We were just half a mile from the mountain summit when suddenly a thick wall of fog rolled in. We couldn't even see our own boots!'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Langsung berasa deg-degannya ya. 'When suddenly a thick wall of fog rolled in!'. Gunakan kata penghubung dramatis seperti 'out of nowhere' atau 'all of a sudden'.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... Let's drill a dramatic storytelling hook."*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Repeat after me: 'Out of nowhere, the entire room fell completely silent.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Tirukan dengan intonasi mendebarkan: 'Out of nowhere, the entire room fell completely silent.' Mantap jiwa!
