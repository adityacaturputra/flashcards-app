## 🎙️ Podcast Script (Full Dialogue)

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Banyak yang mengira Present Continuous itu cuma buat kejadian yang sedang terjadi detik ini: 'I am talking right now'.

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Padahal, penutur asli paling sering pakai Present Continuous untuk rencana masa depan yang sudah terkonfirmasi di kalender.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... Could you explain the difference between 'will' and Present Continuous for future plans?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Gladly, Bima! When you have bought the plane ticket or booked the meeting room, don't say 'I will fly tomorrow'—say 'I am flying to Tokyo tomorrow!' The continuous tense signals that the arrangement is locked in!"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Ingat itu teman-teman: 'will' itu keputusan spontan atau niat umum. Tapi 'Present Continuous' itu jadwal yang sudah fix: tiket sudah dibeli, jadwal sudah disepakati.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Let's practice a confirmed future plan sentence."*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Repeat after me: 'We are launching the beta version next Monday morning.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Tirukan: 'We are launching the beta version next Monday morning.' Pasti dan meyakinkan!
