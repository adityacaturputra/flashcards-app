## 🎙️ Podcast Script (Full Dialogue)

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Selamat datang di Modul 5: Presenting Your Future Dream.

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Semua orang punya impian—ingin bangun startup, studi ke luar negeri, atau menulis buku. Tapi bagaimana menyampaikannya agar tidak terdengar seperti angan-angan kosong?

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... What turns a dream into an inspiring vision?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Actionable clarity, Bima! An inspiring vision connects your passion with a concrete roadmap. Listen to Chloe sharing her aspiration: 'My long-term aspiration is to establish a community tech hub. I am currently partnering with local educators to pilot a digital literacy curriculum by next summer.' Notice how she couples her dream with what she is actively doing today!"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Dua jempol! Chloe tidak cuma bilang 'I want', tapi dia bilang 'I am currently partnering with educators...'. Itu membuktikan dia serius dan sudah mulai melangkah.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... How can our listeners declare their own vision with confidence?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Repeat this empowering sentence after me: 'My ultimate goal is to bridge the educational gap through accessible technology.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Ucapkan dengan penuh tekad: 'My ultimate goal is to bridge the educational gap through accessible technology.' Keren banget!
