## 🎙️ Podcast Script (Full Dialogue)

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Selamat datang di salah satu struktur kalimat paling keren di bahasa Inggris: Future Perfect.

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Strukturnya: 'will have' ditambah Verb 3. Kapan kita pakai ini? Saat kita berdiri di masa depan, lalu menoleh ke belakang melihat apa saja yang sudah tuntas.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... When does the Future Perfect tense shine brightest?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"In milestone planning, Bima! When pitching to executives or setting career targets, Future Perfect shows visionary maturity. Listen to this: 'By December 2026, our team will have automated 80% of routine workflows.' It creates a vivid mental picture of triumph!"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Keren banget! Penanda kuncinya adalah kata 'By' yang artinya 'Menjelang' atau 'Pada saat'. 'By next month', 'By the time you arrive'.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... Let's practice an empowering milestone goal."*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Repeat after me: 'By this time next year, I will have achieved complete fluency in English!'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Tirukan dengan penuh keyakinan: 'By this time next year, I will have achieved complete fluency in English!' Pasti terwujud!
