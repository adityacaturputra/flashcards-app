## 🎙️ Podcast Script (Full Dialogue)

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Masuk ke Modul 7: Plans and Deadlines. Di dunia kerja, deadline adalah realitas harian.

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Bagaimana cara kita mengabari klien atau atasan bahwa deadline perlu disesuaikan tanpa terkesan tidak bertanggung jawab?

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... How should we handle tight deadlines professionally?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Proactivity and transparency are everything, Bima! Never wait until the deadline hour to say you're late. Communicate early, explain the blocker constructively, and offer a concrete revised schedule. Listen to Rachel updating her project lead: 'We are on track with the frontend implementation; however, due to third-party API delays, we may need a 48-hour extension on the final integration.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Bagus sekali. Rachel menyampaikan progres baiknya dulu, menjelaskan alasan objektifnya, dan langsung kasih tanggal pasti perpanjangannya.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Could you share an assertive deadline confirmation line?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Repeat after me: 'Everything is progressing smoothly, and we are well on track to deliver by Friday afternoon.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Tirukan: 'Everything is progressing smoothly, and we are well on track to deliver by Friday afternoon.' Sangat profesional!
