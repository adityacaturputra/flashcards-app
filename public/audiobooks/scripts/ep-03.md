## 🎙️ Podcast Script (Full Dialogue)

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Selamat datang di Modul 2: Stating Your Opinion.

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Seringkah kamu ada di sebuah rapat atau diskusi santai, tapi bingung mau menyampaikan pendapat? Kalau cuma bilang 'I think, I think' terus, rasanya monoton banget.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... How can we make our opinions sound more sophisticated and polite?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Great question, Bima! In professional settings, starting with phrases like 'From my perspective' or 'As far as I'm concerned' immediately elevates your tone. Listen to how Daniel shares his view: 'From my perspective, transitioning to hybrid work isn't just about convenience; it fosters higher creativity and employee well-being.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Dengar betapa elegannya kalimat Daniel. 'From my perspective' langsung memberi sinyal bahwa dia menghargai sudut pandang orang lain, tapi tetap berargumen kuat.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"What about when we feel very strongly about an idea?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Then you can say: 'I am firmly convinced that...' For example: 'I am firmly convinced that sustainable packaging will win customer loyalty.' Repeat after me: 'From my perspective, we should focus on quality over quantity.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Tirukan: 'From my perspective, we should focus on quality over quantity.' Diplomatis dan berkelas!
