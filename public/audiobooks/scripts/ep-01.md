# 🎧 Audiobook Episode 01: Menceritakan Karier & Proyek dengan Mengalir

> **Summary**: Belajar teknik storytelling saat mendeskripsikan pekerjaan tanpa terdengar kaku seperti membaca CV.

---

## 🎙️ Podcast Script (Full Dialogue)

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Hai teman-teman... Selamat datang di Next Level Stories.

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Pernahkah kamu, waktu lagi di sebuah networking event... Tiba-tiba ada kenalan baru yang bertanya seperti ini?

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Hi there! Nice to meet you. So... Could you tell me a little bit about what you are currently working on?"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Nah... Mendadak lidah kita langsung kaku, kan? Kebanyakan orang cuma bilang: 'I am a manager' atau 'I work in IT'. Di episode santai kali ini, Sarah dan aku bakal membagikan cara menceritakan proyekmu dengan mengalir dan percaya diri.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... How would you naturally describe an ongoing project?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Well, Bima... The secret is to keep it authentic. Focus on the impact and the journey! Listen to how Maya introduces her creative initiative: 'So, I am currently leading a project called Project Horizon. We are working to reduce the vulnerability of local craftswomen by helping them design and produce hand-painted bags. We have also been marketing these products across global platforms. It started about a year ago, and it has been showing incredible growth!'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Dengar betapa hidupnya cerita itu. Maya menceritakan masalah yang dia selesaikan, aksi nyata timnya, dan perkembangannya sampai hari ini. Sarah, ada tips pelafalan untuk kata-kata kunci tadi?

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... What are the key pronunciation tips for those words?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Pay attention to 'reduce' — it ends with a smooth juice sound: reduce. Next, 'vulnerability' — break it down: vul-ner-a-bi-li-ty. And 'growth' — end with a soft voiceless th sound: growth. Repeat after me: 'We are working to reduce vulnerability.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Tirukan di rumah teman-teman... 'We are working to reduce vulnerability.' Sekarang satu kalimat lagi, Sarah.

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"'It has been showing incredible growth.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Bagus sekali! Latih kalimat ini saat kamu santai. Sampai jumpa di episode berikutnya!
