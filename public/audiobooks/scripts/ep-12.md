# 🎧 Audiobook Episode 02: Bumbu Kata Penghubung Waktu dalam Cerita Naratif

> **Series**: Next Level Stories (Everyday Professional English)  
> **Format**: Tri-Voice Duet Audio Storytelling (Bima & Sarah)  
> **Audio File**: [02-grammar-usage-for-your-unforgettable-story-in-your-life-audiobook.mp3](./02-grammar-usage-for-your-unforgettable-story-in-your-life-audiobook.mp3)  
> **Summary**: Menguasai 'Meanwhile, As soon as, Gradually, Eventually' untuk memperhalus transisi adegan.

---

## 🎙️ Podcast Script (Full Dialogue)

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Cerita yang bagus bisa hancur kalau transisinya patah-patah: 'And then... and then... and then...'. Bosan banget dengarnya.

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Di episode ini, kita ganti kata 'and then' dengan deretan konektor waktu tingkat dewa.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... What are the best narrative alternatives to 'and then'?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Ditch 'and then' forever, Bima! Instead, use: 'Meanwhile' for parallel scenes. 'As soon as' for immediate reactions. And 'Eventually' for the satisfying resolution. Listen: 'As soon as the storm passed, we set up camp. Meanwhile, the rescue team had dispatched a signal.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Dengar betapa mulusnya adegan berpindah. 'Meanwhile' menggambarkan dua kejadian serentak. 'Eventually' menutup perjalanan.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Let's practice a smooth transition sentence."*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Repeat after me: 'As soon as the news arrived, everyone burst into applause.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Tirukan: 'As soon as the news arrived, everyone burst into applause.' Mengalir dan dramatis!
