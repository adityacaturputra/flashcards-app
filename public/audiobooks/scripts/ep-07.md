## 🎙️ Podcast Script (Full Dialogue)

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Halo teman-teman! Masuk ke Modul 4: Responding to Current Events.

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Waktu ada berita viral atau kabar ekonomi terbaru, bagaimana cara kita menanggapinya di tongkrongan atau forum internasional secara cerdas tanpa terkesan cuma ikut-ikutan?

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... How do thoughtful people comment on current events?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"They balance excitement with critical reflection, Bima. Instead of just saying 'That's crazy!', they balance two perspectives. Listen to Elena reacting to a news report on artificial intelligence: 'I recently read an article about automated logistics. While it certainly boosts efficiency, we must also consider the transitional impact on local employment.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Perhatikan teknik penyeimbangnya: 'While it certainly boosts efficiency, we must also consider...'. Kamu langsung terdengar cerdas dan berpikiran luas.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Could we practice that balancing structure together?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Absolutely! Repeat after me: 'While the innovation is promising, we should also examine the long-term implications.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Tirukan dengan tenang: 'While the innovation is promising, we should also examine the long-term implications.' Sangat matang!
