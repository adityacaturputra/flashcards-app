> **Summary**: Menggunakan 'should, could, might, ought to' untuk memberi saran tanpa memerintah.

---

## 🎙️ Podcast Script (Full Dialogue)

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Di episode kali ini, kita bakal ngomongin kekuatan ajaib dari kata bantu: Modal Auxiliaries. Kata-kata seperti 'must, should, could, might'.

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Banyak orang keliru pakai 'You must do this' ke rekan kerja, padahal itu terdengar seperti komandan militer.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... How does modal choice change the social dynamic?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"It changes everything, Bima! 'You must' sounds demanding and bossy. But if you switch to 'We could consider' or 'You might want to', suddenly you sound diplomatic and collaborative. Let's compare: Bossy: 'You must revise the presentation.' Diplomatic: 'You might want to refine the slide visuals before the client meeting.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Beda banget auranya ya. 'You might want to refine...' terdengar seperti saran hangat seorang rekan yang peduli.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Could you give our listeners a practice drill for this?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Of course! Repeat after me: 'We could explore alternative suppliers to optimize costs.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Tirukan dengan intonasi tenang: 'We could explore alternative suppliers to optimize costs.' Diplomatis dan sangat profesional!
