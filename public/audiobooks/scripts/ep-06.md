## 🎙️ Podcast Script (Full Dialogue)

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Selamat datang kembali! Waktu kita cerita masa lalu, seringkali pendengar bingung: mana yang terjadi duluan, mana yang menyela di tengah jalan?

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Di sinilah trio masa lalu bermain: Past Simple, Past Continuous, dan Past Perfect.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... Can you paint us a picture of how these three work together?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Imagine a movie scene, Bima! Past Perfect is the flashback backstory. Past Continuous is the ongoing background scene. And Past Simple is the sudden action that enters the frame! Listen: 'I had prepared for the scholarship interview for months. On the big day, while I was waiting anxiously in the hallway, the head panelist suddenly opened the door and smiled at me.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Visual banget alurnya! 'I had prepared' itu flashback jauh sebelum hari H. 'While I was waiting' itu situasi yang sedang berjalan di lorong. Dan 'the panelist opened the door' itu aksi tiba-tiba.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"What is the key takeaway sentence for our listeners?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Repeat this smooth narrative sequence after me: 'By the time I arrived, the team had already celebrated the milestone.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Tirukan: 'By the time I arrived, the team had already celebrated the milestone.' Luar biasa!
