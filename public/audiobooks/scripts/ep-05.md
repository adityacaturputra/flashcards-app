## 🎙️ Podcast Script (Full Dialogue)

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Hai semuanya! Masuk ke Modul 3: The Important Year in Your Life.

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Setiap orang pasti punya satu tahun yang mengubah segalanya—tahun kamu lulus kuliah, merantau pertama kali, atau ganti haluan karier.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... Why do personal milestone stories connect so deeply with people?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Because vulnerability and growth are universal, Bima! When you share a turning point, you are sharing your personal evolution. Listen to Alex talk about his pivotal year: '2021 was truly a watershed year for me. I left my comfortable corporate desk to launch an independent design studio. It was terrifying at first, but it taught me resilience.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Wah... Ungkapan 'a watershed year'—tahun titik balik yang monumental. Jauh lebih berbobot daripada cuma bilang 'it was an important year'.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... How should we practice that phrase?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"Let's say it with feeling! Repeat after me: 'That year was a turning point in my personal journey.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Ucapkan dengan penjiwaan: 'That year was a turning point in my personal journey.' Mantap sekali!
