## 🎙️ Podcast Script (Full Dialogue)

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Di episode penutup ini, kita merangkum perjalanan kita dengan Conditionals: kalimat pengandaian.

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> 'Kalau saja dulu aku mengambil kesempatan itu...' atau 'Kalau aku punya waktu tak terbatas...'. Bagaimana mengungkapkannya dengan tepat?

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... Could you break down the conditionals of reflection?"*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"For unreal present dreams, use the Second Conditional: If + Past, would + V1. 'If I had infinite resources, I would build schools around the globe.' For past choices and reflections, use the Third Conditional: If + Past Perfect, would have + V3. 'If I hadn't taken that leap of faith three years ago, I wouldn't have discovered my true calling.'"*

**Bima (Host - ID)**:  
> Ingat rumusnya: Second Conditional untuk khayalan saat ini, Third Conditional untuk pelajaran masa lalu yang sudah terjadi.

**Bima (Host - US English Mode)**:  
> *"Sarah... Let's close our series with an inspiring final drill."*

**Sarah (Co-Host - Native US)**:  
> *"You have made incredible progress throughout this entire journey! Repeat our final practice sentence after me: 'If I continue to practice consistently, I will unlock limitless opportunities.'"*
